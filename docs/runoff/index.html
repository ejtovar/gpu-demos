About Runoff

A port of ryujin's shallow water module to WGSL on a uniform Q1 grid: graph-viscosity low-order update with hydrostatic-reconstruction star states and the guaranteed maximum wave speed, entropy-viscosity-commutator indicator, high-order update, convex limiting of h and |v|² with two limiter passes, affine-shifted bounds, Manning friction, and ERK(3,3,1) time stepping. Two corrections relative to ryujin as written: the full three-case h* in the maximum wave speed, and the exact derivative in the limiter's Newton step. All arithmetic is 32-bit; the time step is reduced on the GPU, so the CPU never waits.

Rain, infiltration and drainage are Strang-split around each ERK step with exact source sub-steps, so depths stay non-negative for any time step. The wind-carried storm transports rain with the same invariant-domain-preserving scheme and hands it to the flood solver every 30 seconds.
