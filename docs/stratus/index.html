Stratus

Compressible Euler equations with gravity (ideal gas, γ = 1.4) solved on your GPU with a WebGPU port of ryujin: continuous Q1 finite elements, graph viscosity with a guaranteed maximum wave speed, an entropy-viscosity indicator, convex limiting of density and specific entropy, and third-order explicit Runge–Kutta. Single precision.

Gravity enters by Strang splitting: half a step of the source, a full Runge–Kutta step of the Euler equations, and another half step of the source. The source step is solved exactly (it changes momentum and total energy but not density or internal energy). An atmosphere at rest is not automatically at rest for the discrete scheme, so it is made so: one step is applied to the undisturbed atmosphere, and the resulting drift is subtracted from every step. The undisturbed atmosphere then stays exactly as it is, and only the terrain disturbs the wind.

The atmosphere is isothermal (sound speed 1) with a uniform wind. Layers at the top, the inflow and the outflow damp the flow toward it, so gravity waves leave the domain instead of reflecting. The terrain is masked out of a fixed grid; the wind slips along it.
